# Sponsor Tracks We're Considering

## Inside and Out: AI for the Built World — by CoStar Group

CoStar Group built the information infrastructure of commercial real estate. CoStar is the platform the industry runs on: proprietary data on 8.5 million commercial properties — leases, sales, tenants and available space — built census-style by the largest research team in commercial real estate and used by the brokers, owners and lenders who move the market. Matterport holds the largest library of 3D digital twins of real buildings. Vexcel captures aerial imagery sharp enough to count parking spaces from the sky. No one else has the inside of the building, the view from above and the market data behind it, all for the same address.

This weekend you get to build on all of it. Bring your skills and an AI agent that uses these layers together to do something new with the built world. That might be for the people who open CoStar every morning — brokers, investors, lenders, developers, appraisers — or it might be a city planner, a delivery robot, a game, an accessibility tool or a classroom. Real estate problems are obviously close to our hearts, but they’re not required; the data is. The best projects will make us say “we didn’t know you could do that with our data.”

Why build here:

The real dataset for Houston, TX and Los Angeles CA, not a sample: CoStar building data, Matterport 3D scans and Vexcel aerial imagery for the same buildings, with your own API keys and a toolkit that exposes all of it via SDKs, MCP Servers and OpenAPI Services
Direct access to the people who built these products — CoStar, Matterport and Vexcel engineers on site and in the Discord all weekend
Spatial and visual by nature — the most fun data you’ll touch this semester
A path forward: finalists get fast-track interviews with CoStar’s AI team, and winning projects get a roadmap review

What we’re looking for:

- Uses at least two of the three layers and gets something out of the combination
- Agentic: reasons, plans and takes action, not a chat wrapper over a property record
- Solves a real problem for a real person — in real estate or anywhere else the built world matters
- Spatial and visual understanding, not only text

Criteria:

- 30% Use of the data — combines layers, uses them intelligently, not as decoration
- 30% Agentic depth — reasons, plans, acts; beyond chat and summaries
- 25% Usefulness — would someone want this switched on Monday? Real estate users are a natural fit, not a requirement
- 15% Demo and polish

## Best Practical AI Innovation — by Amazon

Build AI that solves a real problem for real people. Create AI-powered tools that go beyond demos: applications that reason, plan, take action, and adapt. Whether you're tackling healthcare, sustainability, accessibility, finance, education, logistics, or something entirely new, show us AI that someone would actually want to use every day. 

Key Focus Areas: 
Agentic Behavior: Build AI that can reason, plan, and take multi-step actions 
Real-World Impact: Solve a problem that matters to real users, not just a demo 
Adaptive Intelligence: Learn from context, feedback, or user preferences 
User-Friendly Design: Make powerful AI feel simple and intuitive 
Creative Innovation: Surprise us with unexpected approaches and fresh ideas 

Project Ideas (examples):
A conversational agent that helps users compare products, read reviews, and make purchase decisions through natural dialogue
A logistics or delivery optimization tool that uses real-time data to route, schedule, or predict delays
An accessibility tool that uses voice, vision, or gesture to help underserved users navigate technology
A sustainability platform that turns real-world data (satellite, sensor, public records) into actionable insights
An agentic AI sandbox or learning environment where AI collaborates with you interactively
A personal finance or trading tool that monitors patterns, predicts risks, and coaches better decisions
A group coordination agent that assigns tasks, tracks progress, and nudges teammates on deadlines
A creative AI workflow that turns raw inputs into polished content (music, video, writing, design)
An AI layer on top of existing platforms (messaging, scheduling, browsing) that adds intelligent automation
A multi-agent system that researches and compares options across a domain (housing, courses, jobs, travel, energy) 

Evaluation Criteria: 
Creativity (25%): Is the idea original? Does it approach the problem in a surprising or clever way? 
Usefulness (25%): Does it solve a real problem someone would actually care about?
Technical Implementation (25%): Is it well-built, reliable, and technically sound?
User Experience (25%): Is it intuitive, polished, and something you'd want to use again? 

Project must include:
Working prototype/demo
Clear use case and target user
Basic documentation
Presentation/pitch

Can use any combination of:
Open source AI tools, libraries, and frameworks
API services (LLM providers, search, data, vision, speech, etc.)
Custom models or fine-tuned systems
Agentic frameworks and tool-use integrations
Existing platforms and infrastructure
